# Slide 08: Magnetic Properties (O2)

---

## Diamagnetism vs Paramagnetism

- **Diamagnetic**: all electrons paired → weakly repelled by a magnetic field
- **Paramagnetic**: unpaired electrons → attracted by a magnetic field

---

## MO Explanation of O2 Paramagnetism

- O2 has 12 valence electrons
- The last two enter the degenerate π*(2p) orbitals
- **Hund's rule**: electrons fill degenerate orbitals singly with parallel spin
- Result: **two unpaired electrons** → paramagnetic

---

## Why Lewis Structures Fail Here

- Lewis/VSEPR predicts O2 with all electrons paired → diamagnetic
- Experiment: **liquid O2 is attracted to a magnet**
- Only MO theory correctly predicts paramagnetic O2

---

## Hund's Rule in MO Diagrams

- Degenerate MOs (π_x, π_y) are filled one electron at a time
- Parallel spins minimize electron–electron repulsion
- Governs the ground-state spin multiplicity

---

## Predicting Multiplicity

- Multiplicity = 2S + 1 (S = total spin)
- O2: S = 1 → triplet (^3Σ_g^−) ground state
- This is a celebrated success of MO theory

---

## Other Magnetic Predictions

- N2: all paired → diamagnetic ✓
- NO: one unpaired π* electron → paramagnetic ✓
- CO: all paired → diamagnetic ✓
- F2: all paired → diamagnetic ✓

---

## Summary

- MO theory uniquely explains O2 paramagnetism
- Hund's rule fills degenerate π* orbitals with unpaired spins
- Successfully predicts magnetic behavior across diatomics
- A key limitation of simple Lewis/VB approaches
