# Slide 09: Band Theory Overview

---

## From Molecules to Solids

- As many atoms combine, discrete MO levels merge into **bands**
- Each large molecule's MOs become a quasi-continuous band of states
- Band theory = MO theory applied to the infinite crystal

---

## Bands and Gaps

- **Valence band**: highest occupied band
- **Conduction band**: lowest unoccupied band
- **Band gap (E_g)**: energy separating them
- Gap size determines electrical behavior

---

## Classifying Materials

| Material | Band gap | Conduction |
|----------|----------|------------|
| Metal | Zero (bands overlap) | always conducts |
| Semiconductor | Small (≤ ~3 eV) | can be thermally/topically activated |
| Insulator | Large | essentially none |

---

## Metals

- Partially filled band (e.g., Li, Na): 1s²2s¹ → 2s band half full
- Electrons easily promoted within band → high conductivity

---

## Semiconductors & Insulators

- Full valence band + small gap → semiconductor
- Full valence band + large gap → insulator
- Doping introduces carriers

---

## σ/π Analogy in 1D

- In a linear chain, s orbitals overlap head-on → σ bands
- p_z orbitals → σ bands; p_x/p_y → π bands
- Each atomic orbital produces one band (for one atom per cell)

---

## Energy Bands from MO Ladder

- N atoms → N MOs in a band
- As N → ∞ the discrete levels → continuous band
- Band width reflects overlap strength (like β in LCAO)

---

## Summary

- Bands = dense MO ladders in extended systems
- Band gap dictates metal / semiconductor / insulator
- Core ideas parallel diatomic MO theory (bonding/antibonding)
- Connection to computational solid-state chemistry
