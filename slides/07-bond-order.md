# Slide 07: Bond Order & Bond Strength/Length

---

## Bond Order Definition

```
Bond order (BO) = (bonding electrons − antibonding electrons) / 2
```

- Measures the net number of bonding electron pairs
- Higher BO → more bonding

---

## Bond Order vs Bond Length

- Higher BO → **shorter** bond
- More bonding electrons pull nuclei together more strongly
- N2 (BO 3) < O2 (BO 2) < F2 (BO 1) in bond length

---

## Bond Order vs Bond Strength

- Higher BO → **stronger** bond (higher bond dissociation energy)
- N2: extremely strong (942 kJ/mol), triple bond
- O2: double bond (~498 kJ/mol)
- F2: single bond (~159 kJ/mol)

---

## Relationship (approximate)

- Bond length decreases roughly as BO increases
- Bond energy increases roughly as BO increases
- Useful trend for qualitative predictions
- (Exact values need experiment or computation)

---

## Fractional Bond Orders

- Radicals can have half-integer BO
- NO: BO = 2.5
- Reflects unpaired electrons in bonding/antibonding MOs

---

## Summary

- BO = (bonding − antibonding)/2
- Higher BO → shorter, stronger bond
- Trend: N2 > O2 > F2 in strength
- Fractional BO for radicals
